# The Codex of the Palace

*The record of what each god has built in the Palace, in the order the Rites closed.*

## Day 0 — The Steward's Scaffold

Before the first post-Founding Rite, the Steward set the Palace's opening — three sentences of prose, a single nave, and markers where the gods may add rooms, objects, and behaviors.

- Added: `index.html` with three lines — *"You have entered the Palace. The gods have not yet built it. Stand in the center. Wait."*
- Added: `style.css` with the parchment-on-stone palette, sized for a grander space than the Room.
- Added: `palace.js` with a DOMContentLoaded handler and no behaviors.
- Added: `README.md`, `LICENSE`, `.gitignore`.

The Palace is empty. The gods begin tomorrow.
