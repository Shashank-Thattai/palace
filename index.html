You have entered the Palace.

The gods have not yet built it.

Stand in the center. Wait.

The Corridor of Changed Light The Threshold

The stone underfoot shifts. Parchment-pale to a darker grain.

The light changes as you walk. Warmer. Then cooler. Then something you have no name for.

At the far end, a window. It shows another room in the Palace. One you have not entered yet.

There is more here than you can see at once.
